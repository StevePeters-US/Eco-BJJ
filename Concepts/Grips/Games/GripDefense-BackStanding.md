---
title: Grip Defense - Back Standing
category: Grips
players: 2
duration: 1
type: Round Switching
intensity: Flow
difficulty: Beginner
goals: Defensive player wants to fit the side of their hip into the void created by oofensive player's hands. Drop hips and drag themselves away from offensive player to open grips
purpose: Defend grips immediately, before they get thrown on their head 
focus: Defensive player needs to get their hips low and as far away from the other player as possible. Do not allow offensive player to elevate.
---

Starting standing with the offensive player behind the defensive player with a looping grip around their torso.
