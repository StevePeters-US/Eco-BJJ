---
title: Triangle Defense
category: BloodChokes
players: 2
duration: 1
type: Round Switching
intensity: Adversarial
difficulty: Intermediate
goals: Defender needs to change the angle of their shoulders relative to the attacker's hips. Attacker needs to follow the defender.
purpose: Using movement to prevent a triangle from getting deep
focus: Defender - Square on is to be avoided. Shiftinng to the left or right without falling over, stack them going forward, or back out
Attacker - Mobile hips, keep underneath the defender. Make frames with your forearm to keep you thigh from tipping over when the defender shifts.
