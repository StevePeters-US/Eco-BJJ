---
title: Triangle Defense (Mounted)
category: BloodChokes
players: 2
duration: 2
type: Switch on Win
intensity: Adversarial
difficulty: Advanced
goals: Defender - clear the triangle
Attacker - maintain the triangle. Do NOT apply finishing pressure
focus: Defender - keep at least one carotid open. Off balance the attacker so you can work to a normal triangle which is easier to defend.
Attacker - maintain top position. Your spine should not be in line with the defender.
---

Starting in a locked in mounted triangle. If the locked in triangle is too tight, then an almost triangle with the foot touching the knee will work.
